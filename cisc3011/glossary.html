1. 考卷指令動詞

看到這些字，就知道該寫多少、寫什麼。答錯方向比算錯更慘。

English中文該怎麼答
Compute / Calculate計算要算出數字，並寫出過程
Derive推導要從公式一步步推，不能直接寫答案
State陳述、寫出直接寫出結論即可，不必推導
Explain / Justify解釋、說明理由必須寫為什麼，只寫結論會扣分
Show that…證明…要導到題目給的那個結果
Sketch / Draw畫出要畫圖（座標軸、標示要寫清楚）
Compare / Contrast比較要講兩者的差異，不是各自描述
Briefly簡短地一兩句即可，不要寫長篇
With reference to…根據／參照…答案必須引用題目給的那張圖或數據
Give an example舉例一定要舉具體例子
Determine求出、判定算出或判斷出明確答案

2. 常見英文題幹句型

💡 Part A 多選題的標準問法
  • Which of the following statements is/are correct?
    下列敘述何者正確？（注意 is/are 暗示可能多個）
  • Which of the following is/are TRUE about …?
    關於…下列何者為真？
  • Select all that apply.
    選出所有符合的（明確告訴你是複選）
  • Which of the following is NOT …?
    下列何者不是…？⚠️ 看到 NOT 要特別小心，答案是反過來的
  • …EXCEPT
    「除了…之外」—— 同樣是反向題
⚠️ 英文卷最容易害人的兩個字 NOT 和 EXCEPT —— 通常會用大寫或粗體標出來。 考試時看到立刻在題目上畫圈，提醒自己答案要反過來選。

assume zero padding outside the image假設影像外補零
the origin of the structuring element is at its centre結構元的原點位於中心

各題型的英文結論句（背起來直接寫）

題型英文結論句
均衡化The contrast is enhanced because the concentrated gray levels are spread over a wider range. The histogram is not perfectly flat because gray levels are discrete and some levels are merged.
卷積 vs 相關Convolution requires flipping the kernel by 180°, while correlation does not. Since the kernel here is not symmetric, the two results differ.
中位數濾波The median is more robust because extreme values (0 and 255) are moved to the ends after sorting and therefore do not affect the middle value.
銳化A larger k produces stronger sharpening but may cause halo artifacts, and values outside [0, 255] must be clipped.
SobelSince the gradient is horizontal (θ = 0°) and the edge is perpendicular to the gradient, the edge is vertical.
LaplacianThe edge is located at the zero-crossing, where the response changes from positive to negative.
CannyNon-maximum suppression thins the edge to one pixel wide; hysteresis keeps weak edges only if they are connected to a strong edge, which prevents streaking.
形態學Opening removes small foreground noise while preserving the size and shape of larger objects. Closing fills small holes and gaps.
邊界抽取The morphological gradient is the union of the internal and external boundaries, so it is thicker than either one alone.
⚠️ 寫英文答案的三個原則
  1. 公式和數字比句子重要 —— 英文寫不好沒關係，但算式一定要清楚。
  2. 用 Step 1 / Step 2 標號 —— 閱卷者容易找到你的得分點。
  3. 寫不出完整句子就寫關鍵詞 —— 例如 "contrast ↑, histogram not flat (discrete levels merged)" 也比留白好。
