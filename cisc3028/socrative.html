2Socrative 02：Bill Gross — Why Companies Succeed（創業成功的五大因素）

背景：Bill Gross 的 TED 演講「The single biggest reason why start-ups succeed」
English： Bill Gross (Idealab founder) studied his own and others' startups and identified 5 key factors in startup success: Idea（想法）, Team/Execution（團隊/執行力）, Business Model（商業模式）, Funding（資金）, Timing（時機）. His data-driven conclusion, based on comparing many startups: Timing was the single biggest factor correlated with success (accounting for roughly 42% of the difference between success and failure in his sample), more than team, idea, business model, or funding. Funding was the least correlated with success.

中文：Bill Gross（Idealab 創辦人）研究了自己與其他創業公司，歸納出創業成功的五大關鍵因素：想法（Idea）、團隊/執行力（Team）、商業模式（Business Model）、資金（Funding）、時機（Timing）。他以數據分析得出的結論是：時機（Timing）是與成功最相關的單一因素（在他的樣本中，大約能解釋成功與失敗差異中的42%），比團隊、想法、商業模式、資金都更關鍵；而資金（Funding）則是與成功相關性最低的因素。

Q3 / Q5 是開放式申論題（Justify your answer）：以下整理幾個有代表性的同學論點，可以作為 Part C 申論題的寫作範例：
"Timing is the most important factor. Even with a good team, great ideas, a sound business model and sufficient funding, a startup will fail with poor timing... market timing is a time-sensitive opportunity that cannot easily be re-created." — CHOIIO LEONG
"Bill Gross once developed an online video platform Z.com. It had a solid idea, capable team and sufficient funding, yet failed because it launched too early... household broadband was not widespread and internet speed was too slow." — Leung Cheng Lam（舉 Bill Gross 自己失敗的案例佐證 timing 的重要性）
"Funding is just a starting point. Many new businesses fail without a workable business model... so funding is less important than a strong business model." — Lucas
考試應用： Part C 申論題若考到「哪個創業成功因素最重要」，可以用這個「Bill Gross 五大因素」架構回答：先定義五個因素，再提出自己的立場（通常用 Timing 最容易論述，因為有 YouTube／Z.com／Airbnb 等現成例子可以舉），最後用一個例子佐證——這正好符合 Ch4（opportunity 必須 timely）和 Ch1（necessity vs opportunity entrepreneur）的概念，可以跨章節連結作答。

3Socrative 03：Opportunity、產業分析與定位（4 題）

課堂即時問答 — 全班作答統計
Q1： Which of the following is correct regarding opportunities and ideas? 以下關於「機會」與「想法」的說法哪項正確？｜全班答對率 28/33（85%）
  • a. An opportunity and an idea are essentially the same thing 機會與想法本質上是同一回事0/33
  • b. ✅ It's important to discern whether a particular idea meets the criteria for an opportunity. 重點是分辨某個想法是否符合「機會」的條件28/33
  • c. An opportunity is a thought, an impression, or a notion. While an idea almost always meets the criteria of an opportunity. 機會是一個念頭、印象或概念；而想法幾乎都符合機會的條件（❌ 兩者說反了）2/33
  • d. An idea is a favorable set of circumstances that creates a need for a new product, service, or business. 想法是一組有利的情勢，創造對新產品、服務或企業的需求（❌ 這是「機會」的定義）3/33
Q2： When studying an industry, the following are key questions an entrepreneur must answer before pursuing the idea of starting a firm EXCEPT: 研究一個產業時，以下都是創業者在開公司前必須回答的關鍵問題，但不包括：｜全班答對率 26/33（79%）
  • a. Is the industry a realistic place for a new venture to enter? 該產業是否是新創企業可實際進入的地方？1/33
  • b. Does the industry contain markets that are ripe for innovation or are under-served? 該產業是否包含成熟待創新或服務不足的市場？1/33
  • c. ✅ Is the industry in the maturity stage of the industry life cycle? 該產業是否處於產業生命週期的成熟期？（不是必答的關鍵問題）26/33
  • d. Are there positions in the industry that will avoid some of the negative attributes of the industry as a whole? 產業中是否有位置能避開整體產業的一些負面特質？5/33
Q3： The video industry is becoming less attractive as movie studios perfect the technology enabling consumers to download movies via computer. This is best an example of which industry competitive force? 隨著電影公司完善讓消費者透過電腦下載電影的技術，影片行業吸引力下降。這最能代表哪一種產業競爭力量？｜全班答對率 24/33（73%）
  • a. ✅ threat of substitutes 替代品威脅24/33
  • b. threat of new entrants 新進入者的威脅7/33
  • c. bargaining power of buyers 買方議價能力1/33
  • d. rivalry among existing firms 現有企業之間的競爭1/33
Q4： How a company is situated relative to its competitors is referred to as _____. 公司相對於競爭對手所處的位置稱為＿＿＿。｜全班答對率 18/33（55%，全班最低）
  • a. market share 市場佔有率2/33
  • b. ✅ position 定位18/33
  • c. competitive advantage 競爭優勢（❌ 11 人誤選：競爭優勢是「有什麼勝過對手」，定位是「相對對手所處的位置」）11/33
  • d. brand leadership 品牌領導地位2/33
考試應用： 這 4 題在 Exercises 中也出現過（Q2＝Ex 的 industry key questions EXCEPT、Q3＝threat of substitutes、Q4＝position vs competitive advantage），屬高頻考點。易錯： Q4 全班只有 55% 答對——「position（定位）」是相對對手所處的位置，「competitive advantage（競爭優勢）」是你比對手強的地方，兩者別混淆。Q1 的重點是「想法 ≠ 機會」：想法只是念頭，機會是有利情勢，要先判斷想法是否符合機會的條件。

4Socrative 04：財務工具與籌資（3 題）

課堂即時問答 — 全班作答統計
Q1： Balance Sheet refers to: 資產負債表指的是：｜全班答對率 22/34（65%）
  • a. Showing the funds coming into and going out of your business. 顯示進出企業的資金（❌ 這是現金流量表）1/34
  • b. Showing how much profit or loss is generated. 顯示產生多少利潤或虧損（❌ 這是損益表）2/34
  • c. ✅ Showing assets (what is owned), liabilities (what is owed) and your equity or net worth. 顯示資產（擁有的）、負債（欠下的）與權益／淨值22/34
  • d. All of the above. 以上皆是（❌ 9 人誤選：A、B 分別屬於其他報表）9/34
Q2： Breakeven analysis is a important financial tool that: 損益平衡分析是一項重要的財務工具，它：｜全班答對率 26/34（76%）
  • a. ✅ points at which your gross margin (sales minus cost of sales) equals your fixed operating expenses. 找出毛利（銷售額減銷貨成本）剛好等於固定營運開支的那一點26/34
  • b. compares your company's financial information to the same information from similar companies or to industry norms or ratios. 把公司的財務資料與同類公司或產業標準／比率比較（❌ 這是標竿比較 benchmarking）4/34
  • c. performs a regular analysis of your financial statements such as monthly analysis of profit and loss. 定期分析財務報表，例如每月損益分析2/34
  • d. shows whether you have adequate funds to run and grow the business. 顯示是否有足夠資金經營與發展業務2/34
Q3： Crowdfunding is the practice of funding by: 眾籌（crowdfunding）是透過什麼方式籌資？｜全班答對率 33/34（97%）
  • a. considering a business partner who can put money into your startup. 考慮找能投錢進你初創公司的商業夥伴1/34
  • b. ✅ raising many small amounts of money from a large number of people, typically via the Internet. 向大量的人募集許多小額資金，通常透過互聯網33/34
  • c. using credit cards, but paying off the balance in full every month to avoid quick increase of the total balance. 使用信用卡，但每月全數還清以避免總餘額快速增加0/34
  • d. asking grants from the government. 向政府申請撥款0/34
考試應用： 資產負債表＝資產、負債、權益（某一時間點）；現金流量表＝資金進出；損益表＝利潤或虧損。Q1 有 9 人選「All of the above」，這是典型陷阱，三份報表各有各的功能。損益平衡點＝收入剛好能涵蓋開支；眾籌＝向大量人群募集小額資金（通常透過互聯網）。
