2Socrative 02：Bill Gross — Why Companies Succeed（創業成功的五大因素）

背景：Bill Gross 的 TED 演講「The single biggest reason why start-ups succeed」
English： Bill Gross (Idealab founder) studied his own and others' startups and identified 5 key factors in startup success: Idea（想法）, Team/Execution（團隊/執行力）, Business Model（商業模式）, Funding（資金）, Timing（時機）. His data-driven conclusion, based on comparing many startups: Timing was the single biggest factor correlated with success (accounting for roughly 42% of the difference between success and failure in his sample), more than team, idea, business model, or funding. Funding was the least correlated with success.

中文：Bill Gross（Idealab 創辦人）研究了自己與其他創業公司，歸納出創業成功的五大關鍵因素：想法（Idea）、團隊/執行力（Team）、商業模式（Business Model）、資金（Funding）、時機（Timing）。他以數據分析得出的結論是：時機（Timing）是與成功最相關的單一因素（在他的樣本中，大約能解釋成功與失敗差異中的42%），比團隊、想法、商業模式、資金都更關鍵；而資金（Funding）則是與成功相關性最低的因素。

Q3 / Q5 是開放式申論題（Justify your answer）：以下整理幾個有代表性的同學論點，可以作為 Part C 申論題的寫作範例：
"Timing is the most important factor. Even with a good team, great ideas, a sound business model and sufficient funding, a startup will fail with poor timing... market timing is a time-sensitive opportunity that cannot easily be re-created." — CHOIIO LEONG
"Bill Gross once developed an online video platform Z.com. It had a solid idea, capable team and sufficient funding, yet failed because it launched too early... household broadband was not widespread and internet speed was too slow." — Leung Cheng Lam（舉 Bill Gross 自己失敗的案例佐證 timing 的重要性）
"Funding is just a starting point. Many new businesses fail without a workable business model... so funding is less important than a strong business model." — Lucas
考試應用： Part C 申論題若考到「哪個創業成功因素最重要」，可以用這個「Bill Gross 五大因素」架構回答：先定義五個因素，再提出自己的立場（通常用 Timing 最容易論述，因為有 YouTube／Z.com／Airbnb 等現成例子可以舉），最後用一個例子佐證——這正好符合 Ch4（opportunity 必須 timely）和 Ch1（necessity vs opportunity entrepreneur）的概念，可以跨章節連結作答。
