1AMock Paper 1 — Part A：Multiple Choice（20 題，每題 1 分）

⏱ 建議時間 Suggested time：15 分鐘🎯 20 分滿分📚 覆蓋 Ch1–9
1. An intrapreneur is best described as someone who… 內部創業者（intrapreneur）最準確的描述是： Ch1
  • A. owns and runs a small business independently 獨立擁有並經營一間小型企業
  • B. invests money in other people's startups 投資別人的新創公司
  • C. ✅ works inside an existing organization and uses its resources to pursue a new opportunity 在既有組織內部，運用組織資源去把握新機會
  • D. maintains daily operations without taking initiative 維持日常營運，不主動開拓
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：C
An intrapreneur is an 'inside' entrepreneur: they spot an opportunity and mobilize the organization's own resources. A describes an entrepreneur, B an investor.
intrapreneur 是「內部」創業者：在組織內發現機會，並動用組織自己的資源去實現。A 是 entrepreneur 的描述，B 是投資者。
2. Which of the following is NOT one of the four functions of management (POLC)? 以下哪項「不是」管理四大功能（POLC）之一？ Ch1
  • A. Planning 規劃
  • B. ✅ Marketing 行銷
  • C. Leading 領導
  • D. Controlling 控制
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：B
POLC = Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling. Marketing is a business activity, not a management function.
POLC＝規劃、組織、領導、控制。行銷是企業活動，不屬於管理四大功能。
3. An automated phone menu saves the company money, but most customers prefer to speak to a live agent. This is best described as… 自動電話選單為公司省錢，但多數顧客想找真人客服。這最能形容為： Ch1
  • A. both efficient and effective 既有效率又有效果
  • B. neither efficient nor effective 既沒效率也沒效果
  • C. effective but not efficient 有效果但沒效率
  • D. ✅ efficient but not effective 有效率但沒有效果
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：D
Saving cost = efficient (doing things right). Customers dislike it = not effective (not achieving the right result).
省成本＝有效率（把事情做對）；顧客不喜歡＝沒有效果（沒達到對的成果）。這是課堂上的經典例子。
4. Which view of the entrepreneur emphasizes buying inputs at known prices to sell outputs at uncertain future prices? 哪一種企業家觀點強調「以已知價格買入、以不確定的未來價格賣出」？ Ch1
  • A. Innovator 創新者
  • B. Organizer 組織者
  • C. ✅ Risk bearer 風險承擔者
  • D. Manager 經理
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：C
The three classic views are risk bearer, organizer and innovator. Buying at certain prices and selling at uncertain prices = risk bearer.
三種經典觀點：風險承擔者、組織者、創新者。「確定價格買、不確定價格賣」＝風險承擔者。
5. Which of the following is TRUE about starting a business (reality, not myth)? 以下關於創業哪項是事實（而非迷思）？ Ch2
  • A. Entrepreneurs usually work fewer hours than employees 創業者的工時通常比員工少
  • B. ✅ There is always some form of competition 永遠都有某種形式的競爭
  • C. All expenses can be deducted, so no tax is owed 所有費用都可以扣除，所以不用繳稅
  • D. Entrepreneurs can do whatever they want 創業者可以想做什麼就做什麼
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：B
Myth vs reality: entrepreneurs work longer hours, still pay tax, still have 'bosses' (customers), and still face boring tasks. Competition always exists.
迷思 vs 現實：創業者工時更長、仍要繳稅、客戶就是老闆、仍有枯燥工作。競爭永遠存在，所以 B 正確。
6. Which legal form of business is mainly criticized for double taxation? 哪種法律企業形式最常被批評有「雙重課稅」？ Ch2
  • A. Sole proprietorship 獨資
  • B. Partnership 合夥
  • C. Limited liability company (LLC) 有限責任公司（LLC）
  • D. ✅ Corporation 公司（股份有限公司）
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：D
A corporation is taxed on its income, then shareholders are taxed again on dividends. Sole proprietorship's weakness is unlimited liability; LLC avoids double tax.
公司先被課稅，股東領股息時再被課一次稅。獨資的缺點是無限責任；LLC 則避免雙重課稅。

Accessories model: the core product is cheap/free and profit comes from accessories (e.g. razor and blades).
配件型：本體便宜／免費，靠配件賺錢（例如剃刀與刀片）。

客戶群（Customer Segments）＝企業服務的人群／組織。

10. Which is NOT a rule of brainstorming? 以下哪項「不是」腦力激盪的規則？ Ch4
  • A. Leapfrogging (building on others' ideas) is encouraged 鼓勵在別人的想法上延伸（leapfrogging）
  • B. Freewheeling is encouraged 鼓勵天馬行空

顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：D
The first rule is NO criticism. Freewheeling, quantity/speed and leapfrogging are all encouraged.
第一條規則就是「不批評」。天馬行空、重數量與速度、接力延伸都是被鼓勵的。
11. Bill Gross's Z.com had a good idea, a capable team and enough funding, but failed because it launched before household broadband was widespread. Which quality of a good opportunity was missing? Bill Gross 的 Z.com 有好點子、好團隊、足夠資金，卻因為推出時家用寬頻尚未普及而失敗。缺少了好機會的哪項特質？ Ch4
  • A. Attractive 有吸引力
  • B. ✅ Timely 時機恰當
  • C. Durable 能持久
  • D. Value-adding 能創造價值
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：B
A good opportunity must be timely — not too early, not too late. This is also why Bill Gross rates 'Timing' as the most important success factor.
好機會要「時機恰當」——不能太早也不能太晚。這也是 Bill Gross 認為 Timing 是最重要成功因素的原因（Socrative 02）。
12. Which is NOT one of the six steps for working your business idea? 以下哪項「不是」發展商業想法的六個步驟之一？ Ch5
  • A. Define the market need 定義市場需求
  • B. Research your competition 研究競爭對手
  • C. ✅ Hire your first employees 聘請第一批員工

Six steps: define market need, review personal background, research industry, research target markets, research competition, decide pricing. Hiring is not one of them.
六步驟：定義市場需求、檢視個人背景、研究產業、研究目標市場、研究競爭對手、決定定價策略。聘請員工不在其中。
13. Why is pricing described as an 'ongoing effort'? 為什麼說定價是一項「持續的努力」？ Ch5
  • A. ✅ Market conditions and customer demand change over time 市場狀況與顧客需求會隨時間改變
  • B. Competitors set the price for you 競爭對手會替你訂價
  • C. Prices must never change, to build trust 價格不可改變才能建立信任
  • D. Pricing only matters at launch 定價只在開業時重要

答案 Answer：A
You set an initial price, then adjust it as the market and demand change.
先訂初始價格，再隨市場與需求變化持續調整。
14. Which of the following is a BENEFIT (not a feature) of a smartphone? 以下哪項是智能手機的「利益」（benefit），而不是「特徵」（feature）？ Ch6
  • A. Stores 1,000 phone numbers 可儲存 1,000 個電話號碼
  • B. Has a 6-inch screen 有 6 吋螢幕
  • C. ✅ Gives instant access to family, friends and business contacts 讓你隨時聯繫家人、朋友與客戶
  • D. Contains 8GB of memory 內建 8GB 記憶體

A feature says what the product does; a benefit says why it matters to the customer. Effective positioning sells benefits.
特徵說明產品「能做什麼」；利益說明「對顧客有什麼意義」。有效的定位賣的是利益。
15. A ______ is about the business itself and is meant to stand the test of time, while a ______ relates to a product and is used in campaigns. ＿＿＿與企業本身有關、講求經得起時間考驗；＿＿＿則與產品有關、常用於廣告活動。 Ch6
  • A. logo; slogan 標誌；標語
  • B. ✅ tagline; slogan 口號（tagline）；標語（slogan）

Tagline = about the business, long-lasting. Slogan = tied to a product/service, flexible, used in campaigns. Logo = the visual symbol.
Tagline 與企業本身有關、持久；Slogan 跟產品／服務有關、較靈活、用於廣告活動；Logo 是視覺符號。
16. Breakeven sales are calculated as: 損益平衡銷售額的計算方式是： Ch7
  • A. ✅ Total operating expense ÷ gross margin percent 總營運費用 ÷ 毛利率
  • B. Total revenue − total expense 總收入 − 總費用
  • C. Gross margin percent ÷ total operating expense 毛利率 ÷ 總營運費用
  • D. Assets − liabilities 資產 − 負債
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：A
Breakeven Sales = Total Operating Expense ÷ Gross Margin %. At this point gross margin equals fixed operating expenses. (D is owner's equity.)
損益平衡銷售額＝總營運費用 ÷ 毛利率。此時毛利剛好等於固定營運費用。（D 是業主權益的算法。）
17. Which of the following is a form of DEBT financing? 以下哪項屬於「債務」融資？ Ch7
  • A. Personal savings 個人儲蓄
  • B. A partner's capital contribution 合夥人投入的資本
  • C. ✅ A bank loan 銀行貸款
  • D. Profits retained in the business 保留在企業中的利潤
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：C
Debt = borrowed money that must be repaid, with no ownership given up (bank loans, credit cards). The others are equity.
債務＝借來、必須償還、不涉及擁有權（銀行貸款、信用卡）。其餘三項都屬於股權（equity）。
18. Which of the following is protected by copyright? 以下哪項受版權（著作權）保護？ Ch8
  • A. The idea for a novel 一部小說的構想
  • B. A new operating method 一種新的營運方法
  • C. ✅ The text of a published novel 已出版小說的文字內容
  • D. A natural phenomenon discovered by a scientist 科學家發現的自然現象
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：C
Copyright protects the form/format of a work, NOT the idea, method or discovery behind it. It arises automatically — no registration needed.
版權保護作品的「形式」，不保護背後的想法、方法或發現；且自動生效，不需註冊。
19. How long does an invention patent last? 發明專利的保護期限是多久？ Ch8
  • A. 10 years from application 自申請日起 10 年
  • B. ✅ 20 years from application 自申請日起 20 年
  • C. 25 years from application 自申請日起 25 年
  • D. 50 years after the inventor's death 發明人死後 50 年
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer
答案 Answer：B
Invention patent = 20 years; utility patent = 10 years; industrial design = 25 years (all from application).
發明專利 20 年；實用新型專利 10 年；工業外觀設計 25 年（皆自申請日起計）。
20. At which stage of the organizational life cycle must the founder start working 'on' the business rather than only 'in' it, and begin formalizing policies and procedures? 在組織生命週期的哪個階段，創辦人必須開始「經營」企業（work on）而不只是「在」企業裡做事（work in），並開始制定政策與程序？ Ch9
  • A. Introduction 導入期
  • B. ✅ Early Growth 早期成長
  • C. Maturity 成熟期

Early Growth: sales and complexity rise, so the founder must work ON the business and formalize policies. The five stages: Introduction → Early Growth → Continuous Growth → Maturity → Decline.
早期成長期：銷售與複雜度上升，創辦人須開始「經營」企業並制定政策。五個階段：導入 → 早期成長 → 持續成長 → 成熟 → 衰退。

1BMock Paper 1 — Part B：Netflix Business Model

題目：分析 Netflix 的商業模式，填寫 BMC 九宮格，並指出其模式的優點與缺點（至少 120 words）
1. What is Netflix's business model based on？
Netflix's business model is based on a subscription model: customers pay a regular (monthly) fee for on-demand access to a large library of licensed and original content, which is delivered through its own streaming platform and personalised by data. 以「訂閱制」為核心：用戶按月付費，隨時點播大量授權與自家原創內容，並以數據做個人化推薦。
Block範例答案 Model answer
Value PropositionOn-demand streaming anytime/anywhere, large library + original content, personalised recommendations, (mostly) no ads 隨時隨地點播、豐富片庫與原創內容、個人化推薦
Customer SegmentsMass-market households and individual viewers worldwide, across age groups and interests 全球家庭與個人觀眾（廣大大眾市場）
ChannelsNetflix app and website, smart TVs, game consoles, mobile devices 自家 App／網站、智能電視、遊戲機、手機等裝置
Customer RelationshipsSelf-service, algorithmic personalisation, customer support, easy cancel/switch plans 自助服務、演算法個人化推薦、客服、方便更換或取消方案
Revenue StreamsMonthly subscription fees (tiered plans, incl. cheaper ad-supported plan) 月費訂閱（分級方案，含較便宜的廣告方案）
Key ResourcesContent library and originals, recommendation algorithm and user data, brand, streaming technology platform 內容片庫與原創作品、推薦演算法與用戶數據、品牌、串流技術平台
Key ActivitiesContent licensing and production, platform/software development, data analytics, marketing 內容採購與製作、平台開發、數據分析、行銷
Key PartnershipsStudios/producers/creators, device makers, cloud and payment providers 製片公司／創作者、裝置製造商、雲端與支付服務商
Cost StructureContent licensing and production (largest), technology/cloud and streaming delivery, marketing, staff 內容授權與製作（最大宗）、技術／雲端與串流傳輸、行銷、人事
3. Strength(s)： Recurring subscription revenue gives predictable cash flow, and user data lets Netflix personalise recommendations and decide which originals to make, which raises customer retention. 訂閱制帶來穩定可預測的現金流；用戶數據幫助個人化推薦與決定製作哪些原創內容，提升留存率。

4. Weakness(es)： Very high content costs (licensing and original production), strong competition from other streaming services, and the risk of subscriber churn because customers can cancel easily. 內容成本極高（授權與原創製作）、串流對手競爭激烈，而且用戶很容易取消訂閱，流失風險高。
作答提醒：先寫一句 business model 屬於哪一種（Production／Advertising／Commission／Subscription／Freemium／Accessories），再按九宮格逐格填，最後用「優點＋缺點」各一句，字數即可超過 120。

1CMock Paper 1 — Part C：Essay

題目：選兩個你認為最相關、最有挑戰性、或修課前從未想過的概念，並討論（至少 100 words）
建議寫法（三段式）： ① 定義概念；② 為什麼對你「更相關／更有挑戰／是新觀念」；③ 舉一個具體例子。

範例概念 1：Idea vs Opportunity
English： Before this course I thought a good idea was already a business opportunity. I learned that an idea is just a thought or notion, while an opportunity is a favorable set of circumstances that creates a need for a new product or service — it must be attractive, timely and durable. For example, Bill Gross's Z.com had a good idea, team and funding, but launched before broadband was widespread, so the timing was wrong and it failed.
中文：修課前我以為好點子就等於商機。現在我明白想法只是一個念頭，而「機會」是創造新產品／服務需求的有利情勢，必須有吸引力、時機恰當且能持久。例如 Bill Gross 的 Z.com 有好點子、好團隊與資金，但推出時寬頻尚未普及，時機不對所以失敗。

範例概念 2：Breakeven Analysis
English： Breakeven analysis was the most challenging concept because it links costs, margins and sales in one number. The breakeven point is where revenue is enough to cover expenses (gross margin equals fixed operating expenses), calculated as total operating expense ÷ gross margin percent. For a small coffee shop, calculating it shows how many cups must be sold each month before the shop stops making a loss.
中文：損益平衡分析對我最有挑戰，因為它把成本、毛利與銷售連成一個數字。損益平衡點是收入剛好足以涵蓋費用的水準（毛利＝固定營運費用），公式為總營運費用 ÷ 毛利率。以一間小咖啡店為例，計算後就能知道每月要賣出多少杯咖啡才不會虧損。

2AMock Paper 2 — Part A：Multiple Choice（20 題，每題 1 分）

⏱ 建議時間 Suggested time：15 分鐘🎯 20 分滿分📚 覆蓋 Ch1–9
1. A labor union belongs to which type of organization? 工會屬於哪一類組織？ Ch1
  • A. For-profit organization 為盈利組織
  • B. Nonprofit organization 非營利組織
  • C. ✅ Mutual-benefit organization 互惠組織
  • D. Franchise 加盟組織
顯示答案與解釋 Show answer

2BMock Paper 2 — Part B：Uber Business Model

題目：分析 Uber 的商業模式，填寫 BMC 九宮格，並指出其模式的優點與缺點（至少 120 words）
1. What is Uber's business model based on？
Uber's business model is based on a commission model: it is an intermediary platform that matches riders with independent drivers and takes a cut (service fee) of each ride. It owns the app and network rather than the cars. 以「佣金制」為核心：Uber 是中介平台，配對乘客與獨立司機，並從每次行程抽取佣金；它擁有的是 App 與網絡，而不是車輛。
Block範例答案 Model answer
Value PropositionRiders: convenient, fast, cashless rides with transparent price and tracking. Drivers: flexible income opportunity 乘客：方便快捷、無現金、價格透明可追蹤；司機：彈性賺錢機會
Customer SegmentsTwo-sided: riders (commuters, travelers) and drivers/delivery partners 雙邊市場：乘客（通勤、旅客）與司機／外送夥伴
ChannelsMobile app (main channel), website, app stores 手機 App（主要通路）、網站、應用商店
Customer RelationshipsSelf-service app, ratings and reviews, in-app support, promotions/loyalty offers 自助 App、雙向評分、App 內客服、優惠活動
Revenue StreamsCommission / service fee on each ride (and delivery orders), surge pricing 每次行程（與外賣訂單）抽成、尖峰加價
Key ResourcesApp and matching algorithm, driver network, brand, user and location data App 與配對演算法、司機網絡、品牌、用戶與位置數據
Key ActivitiesPlatform development, matching riders and drivers, pricing, safety and trust, marketing 平台開發、配對乘客與司機、定價、安全與信任機制、行銷
Key PartnershipsDrivers, payment processors, map/navigation providers, insurers, local regulators 司機、支付服務商、地圖導航供應商、保險公司、地方監管機構
Cost StructureTechnology and operations, driver incentives, insurance, marketing, regulatory and legal costs 技術與營運、司機獎勵、保險、行銷、法規與法律成本
3. Strength(s)： The asset-light platform scales quickly because Uber does not own the cars, and network effects (more drivers attract more riders and vice versa) strengthen its position. 輕資產平台，不用擁有車輛即可快速擴張；網絡效應（司機越多吸引越多乘客，反之亦然）鞏固其地位。

4. Weakness(es)： Heavy dependence on independent drivers, exposure to regulation (licensing, worker classification) and price competition in each city can squeeze margins. 高度依賴獨立司機；面臨牌照、勞工身分等法規風險；各城市的價格競爭會壓縮利潤。

2CMock Paper 2 — Part C：Essay

題目：選兩個你認為最相關、最有挑戰性、或修課前從未想過的概念，並討論（至少 100 words）
建議寫法（三段式）： ① 定義概念；② 為什麼對你「更相關／更有挑戰／是新觀念」；③ 舉一個具體例子。

範例概念 1：Features vs Benefits
English： I used to describe products by listing specifications. This course taught me that a feature says what a product does, while a benefit explains why it matters to the customer, and effective positioning sells benefits. For example, "stores 1,000 phone numbers" is a feature, but "instant access to family, friends and business contacts" is the benefit customers actually buy.
中文：我以前介紹產品只會列規格。這門課讓我明白「特徵」是產品能做什麼，「利益」是對顧客有什麼意義，有效的定位賣的是利益。例如「可儲存 1,000 個電話號碼」是特徵，「隨時聯繫家人、朋友與客戶」才是顧客真正想買的利益。

範例概念 2：Challenges of Growth（Cash Flow Management）
English： I had never thought that growing too fast can hurt a business. Growth needs more and more cash for inventory, staff and equipment, so cash flow management becomes a day-to-day challenge, together with quality control and capital constraints. For example, a restaurant that opens three new branches at once may be profitable on paper but run out of cash to pay suppliers.
中文：我從沒想過成長太快也會傷害企業。成長需要越來越多現金來支付存貨、人手與設備，所以現金流管理，連同品質控制與資金限制，成為日常挑戰。例如一間餐廳同時開三間分店，帳面上可能有利潤，卻可能因現金不足而付不出供應商的貨款。
