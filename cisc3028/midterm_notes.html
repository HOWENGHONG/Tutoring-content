1Chapter 1：Entrepreneurship, Organization and Management

1.1 核心定義 Entrepreneurship / Entrepreneur / Intrapreneur
Q：What is entrepreneurship, and what is the difference between an entrepreneur and an intrapreneur?
解題思路／想法：先給 entrepreneurship 的兩個定義角度（設計/launch/run 新業務；承擔風險創造新事業），再用「內／外」區分 entrepreneur 和 intrapreneur：entrepreneur 自己出來創業，intrapreneur 在既有組織內部推動新機會。
English： Entrepreneurship is the process of designing, launching and running a new business, often starting small — or more broadly, taking risks to create a new enterprise. An entrepreneur sees a new opportunity for a product/service and launches a business to pursue it (usually a small business, <100 employees). An intrapreneur is someone inside an existing organization (a researcher, scientist, or manager) who sees an opportunity and mobilizes the organization's own resources to pursue it.

中文：創業精神是設計、推出並經營一間新事業（通常初期是小型事業）的過程；也可以說是承擔風險去創造新事業。企業家（entrepreneur）是自己發現機會並創業的人；內部創業者（intrapreneur）則是在既有組織內部，利用組織資源去實現新機會的人（例如研究員、科學家或經理）。
1.2 常考 Organization — 三種類型
Q：What are the three types of organization, classified by purpose?
English： For-profit organizations exist to make money (business organizations). Nonprofit organizations offer services, such as hospitals, colleges, and social-welfare agencies. Mutual-benefit organizations exist to aid their own members, such as political parties, labor unions, trade associations and clubs.

中文：為盈利組織（為了賺錢，即一般企業）；非營利組織（提供服務，如醫院、大學、社會福利機構）；互惠組織（為了幫助自己的成員，如政黨、工會、商業協會、俱樂部）。
1.3 核心定義 Management & the Four Functions (POLC)
Q：Define management, and list its four main functions.
English： Management is the pursuit of organizational goals, efficiently and effectively, by integrating the work of people through Planning, Organizing, Leading, and Controlling (POLC) the organization's resources.
  • Planning — set goals and decide how to achieve them.
  • Organizing — arrange tasks, people and resources to accomplish the work.
  • Leading — motivate, direct and influence people to work toward goals.
  • Controlling — monitor performance, compare it with goals, take corrective action.
中文：管理是透過「規劃、組織、領導、控制」（POLC）去整合人的工作，有效率（efficiently）且有效果（effectively）地追求組織目標。規劃＝訂目標、想辦法；組織＝安排任務、人員、資源；領導＝激勵、指導、影響員工；控制＝監控表現、對照目標、採取修正行動。
1.4 常考 Efficient vs Effective
Q：Explain the difference between "efficient" and "effective", with an example.
解題思路：記住口訣："Doing the things right"（efficient）vs "Doing the right things"（effective）。選擇題常考「自動語音選單」例子：對公司省錢是 efficient，但顧客體驗差，所以 not effective。
English： Efficient means using resources (people, money, materials) wisely and cost-effectively — "doing the things right". Effective means achieving results and making the right decisions to hit organizational goals — "doing the right things". Example: an automated phone menu is efficient for the company (saves cost) but often not effective (customers prefer a live agent).

中文：「有效率」(efficient) 指聰明、節省成本地運用資源——「把事情做對」；「有效果」(effective) 指做出對的決策、達成組織目標——「做對的事情」。例子：自動電話選單對公司來說有效率（省錢），但常常沒有效果（顧客比較想要真人客服）。

Point 項目Entrepreneur 企業家Manager 經理

1.6 常考 Necessity vs Opportunity Entrepreneurs; 三種 Entrepreneur 觀點
Q：Distinguish necessity entrepreneurs from opportunity entrepreneurs, and name the three classic "views" of an entrepreneur.
English： Necessity entrepreneurs suddenly must earn a living and start a business simply to replace lost income (e.g. after losing a job). Opportunity entrepreneurs start their business out of a burning desire, not because they lost a job.
Three classic views of an entrepreneur: (1) Risk bearer — buys inputs at known prices to sell output at uncertain future prices; (2) Organizer — combines land, labor and capital into a product, profit = what's left after paying rent/wages/interest; (3) Innovator — drives innovation through a new product, a new (untested) production method, a new market, a new raw-material source, or a new form of organization.

中文：「必要型創業者」是因為突然失去收入來源、不得不創業謀生；「機會型創業者」則是出於強烈渴望才創業，不是因為失業。企業家的三種經典觀點：風險承擔者（低價買進、未來以不確定價格賣出）、組織者（整合土地、勞動、資本，剩下的才是自己的利潤）、創新者（透過新產品、新生產方式、新市場、新原料來源或新組織形式來驅動創新）。
1.7 Economic Impact & Management Challenges
Economic impact： Innovation（創造新事物）is central to entrepreneurship; small firms are twice as innovative per employee as large firms. Job creation has shifted toward smaller entrepreneurial firms. 小公司在每位員工的創新產出上，是大公司的兩倍；近年就業機會也往小型創業公司移動。

6 Management Challenges： competitive advantage（競爭優勢）, diversity（多元化）, globalization（全球化）, information technology（資訊科技）, ethical standards（道德標準）, sustainability（永續／綠色經營）— 加上自己的 happiness & life goals。

2.2 Startup Defined & Entrepreneurial Qualities
Startup (Wikipedia)： an entrepreneurial venture — typically newly emerged, fast-growing — that develops a viable business model around an innovative product/service/process/platform; designed to develop and validate a scalable business model. Kinicki & Williams： a newly created company designed to grow fast.
中文：新創公司（startup）是一間新成立、快速成長的企業，圍繞著創新的產品/服務/流程/平台，目標是驗證一個「可規模化」的商業模式；簡單來說就是「設計來快速成長」的新公司。

Qualities（特質）： industry education/experience, strong work ethic, time management, multitasking, management skills, willing to ask for help, self-motivated, resourceful, responsible, organized, persistent, decisive, good health, supportive family. 有些是天生的，有些可以學習，有些是外部因素（例如家庭支持）。

2.5 Business Plan Basics
Definition： a written document describing what a new business plans to accomplish, targeting employees + investors/stakeholders.
3 Types： Summary (10–15 pages，初期測試市場反應) / Full (25–35 pages，用於融資及營運藍圖) / Operational (40–100 pages，內部使用、給營運經理指引)。
4 Basic Contents： Executive Summary（要最後寫，雖然排在最前面）、Section 1: The Business（含 feasibility analysis）、Section 2: Financial Forecasts、Section 3: Supporting Data。
Mission vs Vision： Mission = 為什麼存在／想達成什麼（現在）；Vision = 想成為什麼樣子、想往哪裡去（未來）。

3Chapter 3：Business Model Generation

3.1 核心定義 Business Model vs Business Plan
Q：What is the difference between a business model and a business plan?
English： A business model is the mechanism through which a company generates profit — it describes how an organization creates, delivers and captures value, and its positioning with suppliers/customers/partners. A business plan is a document presenting the company's strategy and expected financial performance, which translates the business model's positioning into concrete actions. The business model sits at the center of the business plan.

中文：商業模式是公司「怎麼賺錢」的機制——描述組織如何創造、傳遞、獲取價值；商業計劃書則是呈現公司策略與預期財務表現的文件，把商業模式的定位轉化成具體行動。商業模式是商業計劃書的核心。

BlockKey Questions

★考試提醒 Exam Tips

1. Part A（MCQ）多為定義型題目，關鍵字要背熟（efficiency vs effectiveness、entrepreneur vs manager、necessity vs opportunity 等）。
2. Part B 常以真實公司（如 Apple）出 BMC 填空題，建議練習：給一間公司名字，30 秒內能說出它的 Value Proposition 和 Revenue Streams。
3. Part C 為申論題，字數通常要求 ≥100 words，建議用「定義 + 為什麼相關/有挑戰 + 舉一個例子」的三段式寫法。
4. 把 Ch1–Ch3 的所有「表格」都轉換成口訣或心智圖，比死記文字更容易在考場上回想。
