1Chapter 1：Entrepreneurship, Organization and Management

1.1 核心定義 Entrepreneurship / Entrepreneur / Intrapreneur
Q：What is entrepreneurship, and what is the difference between an entrepreneur and an intrapreneur?
解題思路／想法：先給 entrepreneurship 的兩個定義角度（設計/launch/run 新業務；承擔風險創造新事業），再用「內／外」區分 entrepreneur 和 intrapreneur：entrepreneur 自己出來創業，intrapreneur 在既有組織內部推動新機會。
English： Entrepreneurship is the process of designing, launching and running a new business, often starting small — or more broadly, taking risks to create a new enterprise. An entrepreneur sees a new opportunity for a product/service and launches a business to pursue it (usually a small business, <100 employees). An intrapreneur is someone inside an existing organization (a researcher, scientist, or manager) who sees an opportunity and mobilizes the organization's own resources to pursue it.

中文：創業精神是設計、推出並經營一間新事業（通常初期是小型事業）的過程；也可以說是承擔風險去創造新事業。企業家（entrepreneur）是自己發現機會並創業的人；內部創業者（intrapreneur）則是在既有組織內部，利用組織資源去實現新機會的人（例如研究員、科學家或經理）。
1.2 常考 Organization — 三種類型
Q：What are the three types of organization, classified by purpose?
English： For-profit organizations exist to make money (business organizations). Nonprofit organizations offer services, such as hospitals, colleges, and social-welfare agencies. Mutual-benefit organizations exist to aid their own members, such as political parties, labor unions, trade associations and clubs.

中文：為盈利組織（為了賺錢，即一般企業）；非營利組織（提供服務，如醫院、大學、社會福利機構）；互惠組織（為了幫助自己的成員，如政黨、工會、商業協會、俱樂部）。
1.3 核心定義 Management & the Four Functions (POLC)
Q：Define management, and list its four main functions.
English： Management is the pursuit of organizational goals, efficiently and effectively, by integrating the work of people through Planning, Organizing, Leading, and Controlling (POLC) the organization's resources.
  • Planning — set goals and decide how to achieve them.
  • Organizing — arrange tasks, people and resources to accomplish the work.
  • Leading — motivate, direct and influence people to work toward goals.
  • Controlling — monitor performance, compare it with goals, take corrective action.
中文：管理是透過「規劃、組織、領導、控制」（POLC）去整合人的工作，有效率（efficiently）且有效果（effectively）地追求組織目標。規劃＝訂目標、想辦法；組織＝安排任務、人員、資源；領導＝激勵、指導、影響員工；控制＝監控表現、對照目標、採取修正行動。
1.4 常考 Efficient vs Effective
Q：Explain the difference between "efficient" and "effective", with an example.
解題思路：記住口訣："Doing the things right"（efficient）vs "Doing the right things"（effective）。選擇題常考「自動語音選單」例子：對公司省錢是 efficient，但顧客體驗差，所以 not effective。
English： Efficient means using resources (people, money, materials) wisely and cost-effectively — "doing the things right". Effective means achieving results and making the right decisions to hit organizational goals — "doing the right things". Example: an automated phone menu is efficient for the company (saves cost) but often not effective (customers prefer a live agent).

中文：「有效率」(efficient) 指聰明、節省成本地運用資源——「把事情做對」；「有效果」(effective) 指做出對的決策、達成組織目標——「做對的事情」。例子：自動電話選單對公司來說有效率（省錢），但常常沒有效果（顧客比較想要真人客服）。

1.6 常考 Necessity vs Opportunity Entrepreneurs; 三種 Entrepreneur 觀點
Q：Distinguish necessity entrepreneurs from opportunity entrepreneurs, and name the three classic "views" of an entrepreneur.
English： Necessity entrepreneurs suddenly must earn a living and start a business simply to replace lost income (e.g. after losing a job). Opportunity entrepreneurs start their business out of a burning desire, not because they lost a job.
Three classic views of an entrepreneur: (1) Risk bearer — buys inputs at known prices to sell output at uncertain future prices; (2) Organizer — combines land, labor and capital into a product, profit = what's left after paying rent/wages/interest; (3) Innovator — drives innovation through a new product, a new (untested) production method, a new market, a new raw-material source, or a new form of organization.

中文：「必要型創業者」是因為突然失去收入來源、不得不創業謀生；「機會型創業者」則是出於強烈渴望才創業，不是因為失業。企業家的三種經典觀點：風險承擔者（低價買進、未來以不確定價格賣出）、組織者（整合土地、勞動、資本，剩下的才是自己的利潤）、創新者（透過新產品、新生產方式、新市場、新原料來源或新組織形式來驅動創新）。
1.7 Economic Impact & Management Challenges
Economic impact： Innovation（創造新事物）is central to entrepreneurship; small firms are twice as innovative per employee as large firms. Job creation has shifted toward smaller entrepreneurial firms. 小公司在每位員工的創新產出上，是大公司的兩倍；近年就業機會也往小型創業公司移動。

6 Management Challenges： competitive advantage（競爭優勢）, diversity（多元化）, globalization（全球化）, information technology（資訊科技）, ethical standards（道德標準）, sustainability（永續／綠色經營）— 加上自己的 happiness & life goals。

2.2 Startup Defined & Entrepreneurial Qualities
Startup (Wikipedia)： an entrepreneurial venture — typically newly emerged, fast-growing — that develops a viable business model around an innovative product/service/process/platform; designed to develop and validate a scalable business model. Kinicki & Williams： a newly created company designed to grow fast.
中文：新創公司（startup）是一間新成立、快速成長的企業，圍繞著創新的產品/服務/流程/平台，目標是驗證一個「可規模化」的商業模式；簡單來說就是「設計來快速成長」的新公司。

Qualities（特質）： industry education/experience, strong work ethic, time management, multitasking, management skills, willing to ask for help, self-motivated, resourceful, responsible, organized, persistent, decisive, good health, supportive family. 有些是天生的，有些可以學習，有些是外部因素（例如家庭支持）。

2.5 Business Plan Basics
Definition： a written document describing what a new business plans to accomplish, targeting employees + investors/stakeholders.
3 Types： Summary (10–15 pages，初期測試市場反應) / Full (25–35 pages，用於融資及營運藍圖) / Operational (40–100 pages，內部使用、給營運經理指引)。
4 Basic Contents： Executive Summary（要最後寫，雖然排在最前面）、Section 1: The Business（含 feasibility analysis）、Section 2: Financial Forecasts、Section 3: Supporting Data。
Mission vs Vision： Mission = 為什麼存在／想達成什麼（現在）；Vision = 想成為什麼樣子、想往哪裡去（未來）。

3Chapter 3：Business Model Generation

3.1 核心定義 Business Model vs Business Plan
Q：What is the difference between a business model and a business plan?
English： A business model is the mechanism through which a company generates profit — it describes how an organization creates, delivers and captures value, and its positioning with suppliers/customers/partners. A business plan is a document presenting the company's strategy and expected financial performance, which translates the business model's positioning into concrete actions. The business model sits at the center of the business plan.

中文：商業模式是公司「怎麼賺錢」的機制——描述組織如何創造、傳遞、獲取價值；商業計劃書則是呈現公司策略與預期財務表現的文件，把商業模式的定位轉化成具體行動。商業模式是商業計劃書的核心。

4Chapter 4：Generating Ideas and Identifying Opportunities

4.1 核心定義 Idea / Creativity / Invention / Innovation / Opportunity
Q：Define idea, creativity, invention, innovation and opportunity, and explain how they relate to each other.
English： An idea is a thought, impression or notion — it may or may not lead to anything. Creativity is the process of generating a novel/useful idea. Invention is the creation of something brand new. Innovation is the process of doing something new/different to create value (can be a new product, service, process, or even a new way of doing business). An opportunity is a favorable set of circumstances that creates a need for a new product/service/business — it is "a situation that presents the possibility of exceeding existing goals". Not every idea is an opportunity — an opportunity must be developed and validated.

中文：「想法」(idea) 只是一個念頭、印象或概念，不一定會有結果。「創意」(creativity) 是產生新穎、有用想法的過程。「發明」(invention) 是創造全新事物。「創新」(innovation) 是做出新的、不同的事以創造價值的過程（可以是新產品、服務、流程，甚至新的經營方式）。「機會」(opportunity) 則是一組有利的情勢，創造了對新產品/服務/事業的需求——即「能超越現有目標的情境」。不是每個想法都是機會，機會需要被開發與驗證。
4.2 常考 Five Steps to Generating Creative Ideas
Q：List the five steps in the creative process.
StepExplanation
1. Preparation 準備Background, experience and education that prepare a person to recognize an opportunity 背景、經驗、教育的累積
2. Incubation 醞釀The time where ideas are mulled over — often while doing something else 讓想法在腦中醞釀（常在做別的事情時發生）
3. Insight 頓悟The "Aha!" moment when the pieces come together 靈光一閃、把片段拼在一起
4. Evaluation 評估Deciding whether the idea is worth pursuing 評估這個想法值不值得追求
5. Elaboration 詳述Moving from idea to actual venture — the hard work stage 把想法落實成實際事業（最辛苦的階段）
口訣： Preparation（準備）→ Incubation（醞釀）→ Insight（頓悟）→ Evaluation（評估）→ Elaboration（實踐）。
4.3 常考 Idea-Generation Techniques
Q：What are the common techniques for generating new business ideas?
Brainstorming 腦力激盪： a group of people, focused on a specific topic, generates ideas spontaneously. 4 rules 四條規則： (1) no criticism allowed 不批評；(2) freewheeling is encouraged 鼓勵天馬行空；(3) quantity/speed is important — move quickly 要快、要多；(4) leapfrogging (building on others' ideas) is encouraged 鼓勵在別人想法上延伸。

Focus Groups 焦點小組： a small group gathered to explore their thoughts/feelings on a specific topic/product — used to generate new ideas and test them.
Library / Internet Research 圖書館／網路研究： a reference librarian can point to industry-specific magazines, trade journals and industry reports.
Customer Advisory Boards 顧客諮詢委員會： a group of actual/potential customers asked to meet regularly to discuss their needs, wants and problems — generates new ideas.
Day-in-the-Life Research 顧客生活體驗研究： company employees spend time at a customer's home/business to observe how the product is actually used, probing for new ideas (e.g. Intuit routinely does this).

中文：腦力激盪——群體針對特定主題自由發想，四原則：不批評、鼓勵天馬行空、要快速產出、鼓勵接力延伸別人的想法。焦點小組——找一小群人探討對某主題/產品的想法與感受。圖書館／網路研究——參考館員可推薦產業雜誌、貿易期刊、產業報告。顧客諮詢委員會——定期邀請顧客討論需求、想要和問題，藉此產生新點子。Day-in-the-life 研究——員工到顧客的家中或公司觀察產品實際被如何使用，藉此發掘新點子。
4.4 Protecting Ideas
English： To protect a new idea: (1) document it properly（properly document the idea — dated notes, records）; (2) secure the idea（legal protections — patents, NDAs, etc.）; (3) avoid disclosing the idea to others without proper agreements in place 避免在沒有適當協議下向他人透露。
中文：保護新想法三步驟：妥善記錄（留下有日期的紀錄）、採取保護措施（專利、保密協議等法律手段）、避免在沒有適當協議前向外洩露。
4.5 核心重點 Essential Qualities of an Opportunity
Q：What qualities make a good business opportunity?
English： A good opportunity is (1) Attractive — customers will actually want/need it; (2) Timely — the window for it is open now (not too early, not too late); (3) Durable — it lasts long enough to be worth pursuing, not a fleeting fad; (4) Anchored in a product/service that creates or adds value for the end user or buyer.

中文：好的機會應具備：有吸引力（顧客確實想要/需要）、時機恰當（不會太早也不會太晚）、能持久（不是曇花一現的流行）、根植於能為最終使用者或買家創造/增加價值的產品或服務。
4.6 核心重點 Three Ways to Identify an Opportunity
Q：Name and explain the three ways to identify a business opportunity.
WayExplanation
Observing Trends
觀察趨勢		Economic forces (disposable income, spending patterns), Social forces (changing lifestyles/attitudes), Technological advances (new tech enabling new products, e.g. Hulu), Political action and regulatory changes 經濟力量、社會力量、科技進步、政治／法規變化
Solving a Problem
解決問題		Opportunities often emerge from recognizing a problem that needs solving 從發現需要解決的問題中找到機會
Finding Gaps in the Marketplace
找出市場缺口		People are frustrated because they can't find a product/service they need, and realize others feel the same way. Example: Daisy Rock Guitars — Tish Cirovolo noticed there were no guitars designed for women, so she built a company around it 當人們因為找不到想要的產品/服務而感到沮喪，且發現其他人也有同感。例：Daisy Rock Guitars 發現市場上沒有專為女性設計的吉他
考試重點： Hulu.com 的機會是由 technological advances（科技進步，讓影片能上傳到網路並播放）所促成；Daisy Rock Guitars 是 finding a gap in the marketplace（找到市場缺口）的經典例子。
4.7 Personal Characteristics of Entrepreneurs Recognizing Opportunity
English： Four personal characteristics that help entrepreneurs recognize opportunities: Prior experience（產業經驗）, Cognitive factors（認知/思考方式、能看見別人看不見的連結）, Social networks（人脈，solo entrepreneurs find ideas on their own, network entrepreneurs find ideas through social contacts）, Creativity（創意）.
中文：幫助創業者發掘機會的四個人格特質：既有經驗、認知因素（能看見別人看不到的關聯）、社交人脈（獨立型創業者靠自己發現想法，網絡型創業者透過人脈發現想法）、創造力。

5Chapter 5：Working Your Business Idea

5.1 核心重點 Six Steps to Develop a Business Idea
Q：List the six steps for working your business idea.
  1. Define the Market Need 定義市場需求 — What problem does my product/service solve? 我的產品/服務解決什麼問題？
  2. Review Your Personal Background 檢視個人背景
  3. Research Your Industry 研究產業（含產業生命週期）
  4. Research Your Target Markets 研究目標市場
  5. Research Your Competition 研究競爭對手
  6. Decide Your Pricing Strategy 決定定價策略
5.2 常考 Industry Life Cycle
English： Stages: Rapid Growth → Maturity → Slow (or Rapid) Decline → Reinvention. Key question to ask: what stage of the industry life cycle is the industry currently in? This isn't about whether the industry is "in maturity" per se, but about whether there are positions in the industry that avoid the negative attributes of the industry as a whole, whether it contains markets ripe for innovation, and whether it is a realistic place for a new venture to enter.

中文：產業生命週期：快速成長 → 成熟期 → 緩慢（或快速）衰退 → 重塑／再造。研究產業時的關鍵問題：這個產業目前處於哪個階段？是否有位置能避開整體產業的負面特質？是否存在適合創新的市場缺口？對新創業者來說是否是個實際可行的切入點？
5.3 Research Your Target Markets
English： Consider the channel position, number of customers, income level, demographics, lifestyle and buying habits of the target market. Data sources： trade associations, reference libraries, government websites, focus groups, surveys. Track trends through newspapers, periodicals, TV and the internet.
中文：研究目標市場要考慮：通路位置、顧客數量、收入水準、人口統計特徵、生活型態和購買習慣。資料來源：貿易協會、參考圖書館、政府網站、焦點小組、問卷調查；並透過報紙、期刊、電視、網路追蹤趨勢。
5.4 Research Your Competition
English： Study competitors' business model, size, location, profitability, market strategy, features/benefits, price and efficiency. Data sources： the competitor directly, suppliers, the competitor's customers, trade associations, the internet.
中文：研究競爭對手的商業模式、規模、地點、獲利能力、市場策略、特色/利益、價格與效率。資料來源：直接向競爭對手取得資料、供應商、競爭對手的顧客、貿易協會、網路。
5.5 常考 Decide Your Pricing Strategy
Q：Why is pricing described as an "ongoing effort"? What approaches can be used to set a price?
English： Pricing approaches include: market price, channel-based pricing (B2B, B2C, distributors, online, affiliate), markup on cost, bundling（offering lower prices when customers buy multiple products/services together）, pricing to the target market, and promotional pricing. Pricing is an ongoing effort — you set an initial price, then adjust it over time, because market conditions and customer demand change over time.

中文：定價方式包括：市場定價、依通路定價（B2B、B2C、經銷商、線上、聯盟行銷）、成本加成定價、組合定價（bundling，買多項產品/服務享優惠）、依目標市場定價、促銷定價。定價是一項「持續的努力」——先訂初始價格，再隨時間調整，因為市場狀況和顧客需求會持續變化。
5.6 Target Market Comparison and Selection
A market segment is a portion of a larger market with common, identifiable characteristics. Entrepreneurs evaluate candidate segments against a set of test criteria to decide which target market(s) to pursue, rather than trying to serve everyone.
市場區隔（market segment）是較大市場中具有共同、可辨識特徵的一部分。創業者會用一套評估標準比較候選的目標市場，選擇要經營哪些市場，而不是試圖服務所有人。

6Chapter 6：Marketing Your Business

6.1 核心定義 Brand & Positioning
Q：What is a brand, and what is "positioning"?
English： A brand is simply the image of your business — what people think of when they hear your business's name. Brand identity is crucial because all marketing activities work to promote/enhance it, building brand recognition. The first step in marketing your business is creating your brand.
Positioning is establishing a position within the market that differentiates the firm from its rivals, by drawing attention to two or three of the product's attributes (e.g. Nike's "Just do it" tagline reinforces its position).

中文：品牌就是企業的形象——人們聽到企業名稱時腦中浮現的印象。品牌識別很重要，因為所有行銷活動都在強化品牌，建立品牌認知。行銷事業的第一步就是建立品牌。定位則是在市場中建立一個與對手區隔的位置，透過強調產品的兩三項特質來達成（例如 Nike 的 "Just do it" 口號強化了它的定位）。
6.2 常考 Selling Benefits Rather Than Features
English： A feature describes what the product does (e.g. "stores 1,000 phone numbers"); a benefit explains why that matters to the customer (e.g. "instant access to family, friends and business contacts"). Effective positioning sells benefits, not just features.
中文：「特徵」描述產品能做什麼（例如「可儲存1000個電話號碼」）；「利益」則說明這對顧客有什麼意義（例如「隨時聯繫家人、朋友、客戶」）。有效的定位是賣「利益」而不只是「特徵」。
6.3 Marketing Message & Marketing Plan
Marketing message 行銷訊息： sums up all elements of the brand in one paragraph (business + target market + products + unique features).
Marketing plan 行銷計劃 包含： target market（規模、消費習慣、人口統計、位置）、product/service（與競爭對手的差異）、marketing communication methods（廣告、公關、文宣品、網路、樣品、促銷）、marketing strategy（訊息、通路/銷售方式、方法與成本）。
6.4 Marketing Methods & Online Marketing
English： Match the marketing method to the target market: B2B product, B2B service, B2C product, B2C service, and sales channels (direct, internet). Major advantage of selling direct： being able to control the process of moving products from origin to end user instead of relying on third parties. Marketing tactics include marketing collateral (business cards, brochures, signage), traditional advertising (print, direct mail, broadcast), and public relations.
Online marketing： a business website builds credibility and helps customers find you; local search (Google/Bing Local); email marketing; online advertising; social media (free but takes time — be real, not too promotional, track your goals/ROI).

中文：依目標市場選擇行銷方式：B2B 產品/服務、B2C 產品/服務、銷售通路（直銷、網路銷售）。直銷的主要優勢：能掌控產品從源頭到終端使用者的流程，不必依賴第三方。行銷手法包括文宣品（名片、傳單、招牌）、傳統廣告（印刷、直郵、廣播）、公共關係。線上行銷：企業網站能建立信譽並幫助顧客找到你；在地搜尋；電子郵件行銷；線上廣告；社群媒體（免費但耗時——要真誠、不要過度推銷、追蹤成效）。
6.5 Logos, Slogans, Taglines & the 4 Ps of Marketing
Logo — a symbol used for public recognition, the "face" of the business. Slogan — related to a product/service, more flexible, used in campaigns. Tagline — about the business itself, meant to stand the test of time.

4 Ps of Marketing (McCarthy)： Product（產品）, Price（價格）, Place（通路）, Promotion（推廣） — also known as the "Product Mix". Lauterborn proposed a 4 C's view: Consumer, Cost, Convenience, Communication.
中文：標誌（Logo）是用來建立公眾辨識度的符號，企業的「門面」；標語（Slogan）跟產品/服務相關、較靈活、常用於廣告活動；口號（Tagline）則跟企業本身有關，講求經得起時間考驗。行銷 4P：產品、價格、通路、推廣，又稱「產品組合」；Lauterborn 提出對應的 4C：消費者、成本、便利性、溝通。

7Chapter 7：Financing Your Business

7.1 核心重點 Financial Planning — Why & the 5 Elements
Q：Why is financial planning essential to a startup, and what are the five elements of a financial plan?
Why 為什麼重要： (1) figure out how much money is needed to start/run the business; (2) create a plan to guide decisions and monitor results; (3) communicate with stakeholders (investors, lenders, employees) about the business's finances（注意：是「finances」不是「profits」，這是常見陷阱題）; (4) better understand financial risks and rewards.

5 Elements 五大要素： (1) Required Startup Funds（含 6–18 個月的週轉金）; (2) Salaries and Wages（含稅，附加福利約佔薪資15%）; (3) Fixed Operating Expenses（保險、租金、水電、佣金、廣告、稅、執照等）; (4) Projected Sales Forecast（含銷售單位預測與毛利率 gross profit margin）; (5) Cash Receipts and Disbursements（付款條件通常為0–30/31–60/60+天）。
7.2 常考 Three Financial Statements
Statement 報表Shows 顯示
Income Statement
損益表		How much profit/loss is generated; helps manage overhead and shows time to profitability 顯示獲利或虧損、幫助管理費用、顯示達到獲利所需時間
Balance Sheet
資產負債表		Assets（what is owned）, Liabilities（what is owed）, Equity/net worth 資產、負債、業主權益
Cash Flow Statement
現金流量表		Funds coming into and going out of the business — the exact opposite of a budget 現金流入與流出——與「預算」正好相反
7.3 Breakeven Analysis & Benchmarking
Breakeven point 損益兩平點： the point where revenue coming into the firm is sufficient to cover expenses (gross margin = fixed operating expenses). Formula: Breakeven Sales = Total Operating Expense ÷ Gross Margin Percent.
Benchmarking 標竿比較： comparing your company's financial data to industry norms/similar companies, to test the feasibility of your financial plan.
中文：損益兩平點是公司收入恰好足以涵蓋費用的那一點（毛利 = 固定營運費用）。公式：損益平衡銷售額 = 總營運費用 ÷ 毛利率。標竿比較是把自己公司的財務數據與產業標準/同業比較，用來測試財務計劃的可行性。
7.4 核心重點 Sources of Capital
Q：Distinguish equity vs debt financing, and name the Six C's of Credit banks look for.
Equity 股權（signifies ownership）： personal savings, investments from family/friends, partners' contributions, profits retained in the business.
Debt 債務（borrowed, must be repaid, no ownership）： bank/credit union loans, Community Express Micro Loans, credit cards.
Other sources 其他來源： development agencies, angel investors（individuals, often former entrepreneurs, who invest in promising companies）, venture capitalists（invest in high-growth firms for rapid ROI）, peer-to-peer loans, crowdfunding（raising small amounts from many people, typically online）. Grants 補助金： mostly only available to nonprofit organizations, not typical for-profit startups.

Six C's of Credit 信用六要素： Character（信用與誠信紀錄）, Capacity（還款能力）, Capital（業主個人投入的資金）, Collateral（可作抵押的有形資產，如不動產、設備）, Conditions（貸款條件、產業/景氣狀況）, Cash Flow（還款資金的來源）。

8Chapter 8：Get to Know Intellectual Property

8.1 核心定義 What is Intellectual Property?
English： Intellectual property (IP) is property generated through intellectual activities — the product of human imagination, creativity and inventiveness. It is intangible but, like tangible property, is protected by law. Main components: copyrights, patents, trademarks. IP needs protection because it ensures creators/inventors are rewarded, which encourages continued creative work and drives economic development.
中文：智慧財產權是人類智力活動（想像力、創造力、發明能力）產生的財產。它是無形的，但和有形財產一樣受法律保護。主要包含：著作權、專利、商標。保護智慧財產權的理由：確保創作者/發明者能獲得回報，藉此鼓勵持續創作，推動經濟發展。
8.2 常考 Copyright 著作權
Protects 保護： the form/format of a creative piece (literary, artistic, music, software, photography, architecture, etc.) — NOT the idea, method, opinion or fact behind it.
Not protected 不受保護： ideas, programs, operating methods, rules, concepts, discoveries, information for public use, daily news, political speeches, laws and regulations.
Registration 註冊： automatic from the moment the piece is created — no registration required.
Co-ownership 共同擁有： a piece created by a team is co-owned by all team members.
Duration 期限： generally 50 years after the creator's death; for co-owned works, 50 years after the death of the last creator; for anonymous/undisclosed-author works, 50 years after publication.

中文：著作權保護的是作品的「形式」，不是背後的想法、方法、意見或事實。不受保護的包括：想法、程式、營運方法、規則、概念、發現、供公眾使用的資訊、每日新聞、政治演說、法律法規。著作權自作品完成那一刻起自動生效，不需要註冊。團隊共同創作的作品由全體成員共同擁有。保護期限：一般為創作者死後50年；共同著作為最後一位創作者死後50年；匿名/未公開作者身分的作品則為發表後50年。
8.3 常考 Patent 專利
English： A patent gives the owner of an invention the exclusive right to exploit it for a limited period; it can be transferred/succeeded during its period of validity. Anyone using a patented product/method must obtain a license from the owner.
3 types 三種類型： Invention patent（new technical schemes for products/methods, duration 20 years from application）; Utility patent（new technical schemes for shape/structure, lower technical level than invention patents, duration 10 years）; Industrial model & design（protects shape/pattern/color of a product, aesthetic + industrially applicable, duration 25 years）.
Not patentable 不能申請專利： discoveries（no creative element, e.g. discovering a substance/phenomenon）; designs that only serve a specified function (e.g. a USB plug) are not eligible for industrial design registration.
Business method patent 商業方法專利： applies to things like Amazon's one-click ordering or Priceline's "name-your-own-price" model.

中文：專利賦予發明所有人在限定期間內獨家利用發明的權利；在有效期內可轉讓或繼承。任何人使用受專利保護的產品或方法都必須取得所有人授權。三種類型：發明專利（新技術方案，申請日起20年）、實用新型專利（形狀/結構的新技術方案，技術層級較低，申請日起10年）、工業外觀設計（保護產品的形狀/圖案/顏色，須兼具美感與工業應用性，申請日起25年）。「發現」不能申請專利（不含創造性元素，例如發現一種物質或現象）；只具備特定功能的設計（如USB插頭）不能登記為工業外觀設計。商業方法專利：適用於像Amazon一鍵下單、Priceline「自訂價格」這類模式。
8.4 Trademark 商標 & Trade Secrets 商業機密
Trademark 商標： a sign (words, symbols, colors, 3D shapes, even sounds) used to distinguish one company's goods/services from another's. Key difference from industrial design: a trademark does not require the good itself as the protected subject (it protects the sign), while industrial design protects the product's whole/partial appearance.
Trade secret 商業機密： any formula, pattern, device, idea or process that gives a competitive advantage (e.g. marketing plans, financial forecasts, product formulas). Does not require formal registration — protected by keeping it confidential on a "need-to-know" basis, restricting access, labeling documents, etc. Example: a VP who leaves and reuses an identical marketing plan at a rival firm is a trade secret dispute.

中文：商標是用來區別不同企業商品/服務的標誌（文字、符號、顏色、立體形狀甚至聲音）。與工業外觀設計的關鍵差異：商標不需要以商品本身作為保護對象（保護的是標誌），而工業外觀設計保護的是產品整體或部分的外觀。商業機密是任何能帶來競爭優勢的配方、模式、裝置、想法或流程（例如行銷計劃、財務預測、產品配方），不需要正式註冊，而是靠限制知情範圍（need-to-know）、限制存取、標示文件等方式保密。例如：某副總離職後在對手公司複製幾乎一模一樣的行銷計劃，屬於「商業機密糾紛」。

9Chapter 9：The Challenges of Growth

9.1 Preparing for Growth
English： Not all businesses have the potential to be aggressive-growth firms; a business can grow too fast. Firms should (1) appreciate the nature of business growth, (2) stay committed to a core strategy（not chase every opportunity）, and (3) plan for growth（writing a business plan helps）.
中文：不是每個企業都有能力快速擴張，企業也可能成長得太快。企業應該：理解成長的本質、堅守核心策略（不要盲目追逐每個機會）、為成長做規劃（撰寫商業計劃書有助於此）。
9.2 常考 Stages of Growth（Organizational Life Cycle）
Stage 階段Key Point
I. Introduction 導入期Start-up phase; determine core strengths; make sure the initial product/service is right 確認核心強項、確保最初的產品/服務是對的
II. Early Growth 早期成長Increasing sales + complexity; founder must work "on" the business, not just "in" it; formalize policies/procedures 要開始「經營」而不只是「在」企業裡做事；開始制定政策程序
III. Continuous Growth 持續成長More structure/formalization; often develops related products; toughest decisions (can current management take it further?) 更多結構化；常開發相關產品；最難的決策階段
IV. Maturity 成熟期Growth stalls; focus shifts to managing efficiently; look for partnering/acquisition/licensing to breathe new life 成長停滯、重心轉向有效率地管理；尋找合作/收購/授權機會
V. Decline 衰退期Not inevitable — avoidance depends on leadership strength and adaptability 並非不可避免——能否避免取決於領導力與適應力
考試重點： 「Reinvention（重塑）」不是組織生命週期的一個階段（這是常見陷阱選項）——生命週期是 Introduction → Early Growth → Continuous Growth → Maturity → Decline。
9.3 Reasons for Growth
Economies of scale 規模經濟： increasing production lowers the average cost per unit（例：生產越多，單位成本越低）。Economies of scope 範疇經濟： the scope/range of a firm's operations creates efficiencies。Market leadership 市場領導地位： holding the #1 or #2 position in an industry/niche by sales volume。Influence, Power & Survivability： larger firms have more influence。Accommodating key customers' growth： grow to keep up with a key customer。Attracting/retaining talent： growth creates promotion opportunities。
9.4 核心重點 Challenges of Growth
Managerial Capacity Problem 管理能力問題： firm growth requires turning entrepreneurial services（辨識新機會）into managerial services（執行日常與新機會），but the ability to increase managerial capacity is constrained by socialization of new managers, managerial motivation, adverse selection, and moral hazard.
Day-to-Day Challenges 日常挑戰： Cash Flow Management（成長需要越來越多現金）, Price Stability（搶市佔可能引發價格戰）, Quality Control（活動量增加但資源跟不上，品質就會出問題）, Capital Constraints（資金限制始終存在）。

中文：管理能力問題：企業成長需要把「發現新機會的創業能力」轉化成「執行日常與新機會的管理能力」，但管理能力的提升會受到新經理人社會化、管理動機、逆選擇、道德風險等因素限制。日常挑戰：現金流管理（成長需要越來越多現金）、價格穩定（搶佔市佔可能引發價格戰）、品質控制（業務量增加但資源跟不上就會出問題）、資金限制（持續存在的問題）。
9.5 常考 Internal vs External Growth Strategies
TypeIncludesAdvantageDisadvantage
Internal 內部成長New product development, other product-related strategies（市場滲透、延伸產品線、地理擴張）, international expansion（出口、授權、合資、加盟、交鑰匙工程、獨資子公司）Incremental control; preserves culture; allows promotion from within 漸進可控、保留企業文化、可內部晉升Slow; needs new resources; adds to industry capacity 較慢、需要新資源、增加產業產能
External 外部成長Mergers & Acquisitions（合併/收購）, Licensing（授權）, Strategic Alliances & Joint Ventures（策略聯盟/合資）, Franchising（加盟）Access to new products/markets/tech/brand; economies of scale; diversifies risk 快速取得新產品/市場/技術/品牌、規模經濟、分散風險Culture clash; operational complexity; loss of flexibility; antitrust issues 文化衝突、營運複雜、失去彈性、可能涉及反壟斷
定義： A merger pools two+ firms into one; an acquisition is the outright purchase of one firm by another. A strategic alliance is an informal partnership for a specific goal (no new entity created); a joint venture creates a new, separate jointly-owned entity.

★考試提醒 Exam Tips

1. Part A（MCQ）多為定義型題目，關鍵字要背熟（efficiency vs effectiveness、entrepreneur vs manager、necessity vs opportunity、observing trends vs solving a problem vs finding gaps 等）。
2. Part B 常以真實公司（如 Apple、Skype）出 BMC 填空題，建議練習：給一間公司名字，30 秒內能說出它的 Value Proposition 和 Revenue Streams。
3. Part C 為申論題，字數通常要求 ≥100 words，建議用「定義 + 為什麼相關/有挑戰 + 舉一個例子」的三段式寫法。
4. 把每章的所有「表格」都轉換成口訣或心智圖，比死記文字更容易在考場上回想。
5. 特別留意常見陷阱選項：financial planning 是為了溝通 finances（不是 profits）；breakeven point 是「revenue 足以涵蓋 expenses」而不是「開始獲利」；organizational life cycle 沒有 "Reinvention" 這個階段。
