Section F — Sentence Rearrangement(20% @5)

Rearrange the following words or phrases to form a complete sentence. Change the forms of the words and add extra words where necessary. 排列句子，可改變詞形及加字。

1. Assist (somebody) in/with something/doing something
( the librarian / me / with / find / a book / assisted )
參考答案: The librarian assisted me with finding a book.
2. Be interested in somebody/something/doing something
( my classmates / are / in / joining / the robotics club / interested )
參考答案: My classmates are interested in joining the robotics club.
3. Revise for something
( we / for / the coming test / need / revise / to )
參考答案: We need to revise for the coming test.
4. Protect somebody/something from somebody/something/doing something
( sunscreen / our skin / harmful UV rays / protects / from )
參考答案: Sunscreen protects our skin from harmful UV rays.
